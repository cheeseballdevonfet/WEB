Metis: intelligence in motion

A 15-second showreel in the manner of a 1930s light play. A blue beam turns on and a rotating perforated disc throws moving points of light that spell METIS. A sculpture of rods and spheres swings into the light; its shadow tumbles, then locks into the Metis mark, and a white light comes on. The five sphere-shadows turn into five spotlights that light the five capabilities: Enterprise AI, Agentic Systems, Workflow Automations, AI Strategy and Validation, and Product Engineering. The spotlights converge into one light; five ghost shadows align into the mark beside the words METIS, Intelligence in motion, metisai.in.
