## Connect

### SMS Blasting
**Line (deck):** Bulk SMS campaigns on DLT-registered templates.

**What we do:**
- Registration on the DLT platform: your entity, sender IDs (headers) and message templates, as TRAI's rules for commercial SMS require.
- Promotional, transactional and service messages, each sent on the right route.
- Audience lists cleaned, segmented and checked against DND preferences for promotional sends.
- Messages written short and clear, with a link or a missed-call or WhatsApp action.
- Delivery and click reporting in the same dashboard as your other channels.

**Good for:** offers, reminders, alerts, event invitations, payment and appointment notices.
**Works with:** WhatsApp Blasting, AI Calling, Live reporting.

**Questions:**
- *Do I need DLT registration?* Yes, every business sending commercial SMS in India must register. We handle the process with you. **[confirm]**
- *Can I send to anyone?* Promotional messages go only to numbers that are allowed to receive them, at permitted times. We set this up correctly from day one.

### WhatsApp Blasting
**Line (deck):** Broadcast offers and updates where customers actually read.

**What we do:**
- Set-up on the official WhatsApp Business Platform with your verified business profile.
- Message templates written and submitted for approval.
- Broadcasts only to customers who have opted in, segmented by interest, city or stage.
- Rich messages: images, documents, buttons and quick replies.
- Replies handed to your team or to the WhatsApp Chat Bot, so a broadcast becomes a conversation.

**Good for:** offers, launches, catalogues, reminders, festive campaigns.
**Works with:** WhatsApp Chat Bot, SMS Blasting, Post Creation & Content.

**Questions:**
- *Why do customers need to opt in?* WhatsApp only allows business messages to people who have agreed to receive them. Respecting that keeps your number in good standing and your messages welcome.

### WhatsApp Chat Bot
**Line (deck):** 24×7 automated replies, lead capture and support.

**What we do:**
- Conversations designed around your top questions: prices, timings, locations, eligibility, bookings.

- Bookings and callbacks scheduled inside the chat.

**Good for:** clinics, coaching centres, real-estate projects, insurers, retailers, anyone whose phone rings with the same questions.
**Works with:** WhatsApp Blasting, AI Calling, Website Development.

**Questions:**

- *Which languages?* English, Hindi and other Indian languages. **[confirm]**
