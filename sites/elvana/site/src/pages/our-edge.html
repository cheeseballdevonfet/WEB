Rules we keep

Automation only works when people trust it. These hold on every campaign.

  • Opt-ins respected.

    WhatsApp broadcasts go only to customers who have agreed to hear from you.

  • DLT registration for SMS.

    Your entity, sender IDs and templates, registered as TRAI's rules for commercial SMS require.

  • Automated agents say so.

    The bot and the AI caller introduce themselves as automated.

  • A human is one step away.

    Any customer can ask for a person, at any point.
