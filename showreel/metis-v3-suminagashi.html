Metis: intelligence in motion

A 15-second showreel made as suminagashi, Japanese ink marbling, on warm washi paper in black sumi ink and Metis blue. A single blue drop lands and blooms into concentric rings. Five drops land where the five circles of the Metis mark sit and grow into ring systems that press against each other; ink lines join them into the Metis mark, beside the five capabilities: Enterprise AI, Agentic Systems, Workflow Automations, AI Strategy and Validation, Product Engineering. A comb drags slowly across the bath, pulling the mark into a flowing marbled pattern and clearing the space where the line "Intelligence in motion." appears. A last, large clear drop opens the page beside the finished marbled print: METIS, Intelligence in motion., metisai.in and the Metis mark.
