What we do

AI voice agents for follow-ups, reminders and qualification.

  • Voice agents in Indian languages for lead follow-ups, appointment reminders, renewals and feedback.
  • Scripts written and tested with your team before any call goes out.
  • Qualification questions that sort hot leads from later ones, with notes for your team.
  • Hand-off to a human caller or a WhatsApp conversation when needed.
  • Calls placed within telecom rules and DND preferences, with the agent saying it is an automated assistant.
