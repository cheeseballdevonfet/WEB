What we do

Bulk SMS campaigns on DLT-registered templates.

  • Registration on the DLT platform: your entity, sender IDs (headers) and message templates, as TRAI's rules for commercial SMS require.
  • Promotional, transactional and service messages, each sent on the right route.
  • Audience lists cleaned, segmented and checked against DND preferences for promotional sends.
  • Messages written short and clear, with a link or a missed-call or WhatsApp action.
  • Delivery and click reporting in the same dashboard as your other channels.

Questions

Do I need DLT registration?

Yes. Businesses sending commercial SMS in India register on the DLT platform, and we work through the process with you.

Can I send to anyone?

Promotional messages go only to numbers that are allowed to receive them, at permitted times. We set this up correctly from day one.

Can this be a retainer, a campaign or a project?
