You have questions, we have answers

Clear, direct answers to the questions we hear most from founders and operations teams.

What does Metis actually do?

We're an AI product studio and consultancy. We help teams figure out where AI pays off, then design, build, and ship it, from a first prototype to production software, across five capabilities: Enterprise AI, Agentic Systems, Workflow Automations, AI Strategy & Validation, and Product Engineering.

We're not sure where to start. Can you help?

Yes, that's exactly what the Discovery Sprint is for. In two weeks (₹2L / $2k, fixed) we run a goals workshop, recommend an architecture and stack, and hand you a prioritized, ROI-ranked roadmap you can act on, with or without us.

Who do you work with?

Business owners and startups who want AI to move the needle, plus established teams automating workflows or building custom internal tools. We go deep in regulated, high-stakes industries like finance, healthcare, and real estate, but we're not limited to them.

How do you price engagements?

Two main ways beyond the Discovery Sprint: a fixed-cost project when scope is well-defined, or an embedded team that plugs into yours and ships alongside you. We shape the model around your problem, with no surprises on cost.

How fast can you deliver something we can see?

We usually put a working prototype in front of you within 2 to 4 weeks. We prioritize the riskiest assumption first, validate it quickly, then scale what works into production.

Do you build production systems, or just prototypes?

Both, but the goal is always production. We build software with compliance, security, and observability designed in from day one, so what we ship holds up under real usage and real scrutiny.

What happens after launch?

We don't hand over a demo and disappear. We can monitor, maintain, and keep extending what we built, and we make sure your team actually knows how to run and grow it.
