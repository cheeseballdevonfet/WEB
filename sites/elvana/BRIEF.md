# Elvana Media website: brief and three directions

Built with the Creative Prompt Kit's `creative-web` skill (`creative-motion-kit/skill/creative-web/SKILL.md`) and `WEB.md`. The master brief is W01; the method for comparing options is the "Three radically different directions" remix. All three variations use **the same content** (`CONTENT.md`) and **the same section order** so they can be compared fairly. Only the art direction, type, palette, layout system and interaction model differ.

## Interview answers (from the brand deck)

1. **Visitors and what they must believe.** Founders, marketing heads and business owners in India, from startups and D2C brands to corporates, with a lean toward insurance, healthcare, education and real estate. By the end they must believe that Elvana is one partner that can make them seen, chosen and grown. It joins creative work with AI automation that keeps working, and it is run with a regulated group's discipline.
2. **What they meet first.** "Ideas that create impact." plus a live demonstration that Elvana's ideas actually reach people.
3. **Real assets.**
   - The deck's copy: services, pillars, process, models, team, group, cities and contact.
   - A navy and gold raster logo, for reference only.
   - There are no photos, case studies, testimonials or prices.
4. **Admired sites.** None were given. Use references from outside web design (below).
5. **What this site does that others don't.** It shows reach instead of claiming it. The visitor watches an idea land, travel or make an impact.
6. **What must never appear.**
   - Invented clients, logos, results, testimonials or prices.
   - Stock or AI photos of people.
   - The ALL OUT ban list (October 2026).

**Design read.** Reading this as an agency homepage for Indian founders and marketing heads, with a confident, kinetic, street-smart language, leaning toward print and broadcast media made physical.

## Section order (identical in all three)

1. **Header.** The "Elvana" wordmark and links to Services, Our edge, How we work, About and Contact, plus a primary action, "Let's talk".
2. **First screen.** "Ideas that create impact." with the positioning line and the call to action. The signature interaction lives here.
3. **Who we are.** The four real facts.
4. **Create. Connect. Grow.** The three pillars, then the 8 core services and the 6 "beyond digital" services.
5. **Our edge: where media meets AI.** AI calling, WhatsApp bot, blasting and live reporting, demonstrated rather than described.
6. **How we work.** Five steps.
7. **Who we serve.** Eight sectors, plus the regulated-category head start.
8. **Work.** An honest, designed "first case studies are being written" state with a call to action.
9. **Engagement models.** Three models, with price TODOs.
10. **People and backing.** The founder, the leadership team and the BeyondSure group.
11. **Cities.** Four cities and "next".
12. **Contact and footer.** A form that degrades to mailto and the real details, then the legal line in the footer.

## Shared rules

- **Stack.** One self-contained HTML file with no external requests at runtime. Embed the fonts as base64 woff2 subsets. The subset must include ₹; use the Google Fonts `text=` parameter for missing glyphs. Use vanilla JS, with libraries only if they're inlined and earn their bytes. Stay under 500 KB.
- **Kit power-ups that apply.** Design system first, ALL OUT (web) with the full October 2026 ban list, Motion review switch (a quiet footer toggle plus the S key, off by default), Juror critique and Ship-ready checks.
- **Content.** Use `CONTENT.md` only.
- **Wordmark.** A typographic wordmark only. Never redraw or trace the official logo.
- **Mobile.** Designed, not shrunk. The signature interaction must work by touch.
- **Reduced motion.** A finished, still version.
- **No JavaScript.** All content readable.
